## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

# Direct Farm – Smart Digital Marketplace for Farmers

## 1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution

Direct Farm is a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with customers. Farmers can register, upload their agricultural products, and update prices. Customers can view available products, compare prices, and place orders online.

## 2. How It Addresses the Problem

Farmers often depend on middlemen to sell their products, which reduces their earnings and increases prices for consumers.

Our project reduces the involvement of middlemen by connecting farmers directly with customers. This helps farmers earn better profits and allows customers to purchase fresh products at affordable prices.

## 3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution

- Direct connection between farmers and customers.
- Easy online product listing and ordering.
- Transparent product pricing.
- Reduced dependency on intermediaries.
- Future integration of AI for demand forecasting and delivery route optimization.

## Technical Approach

## 1. Technologies to Be Used

- **HTML:** Website structure.
- **CSS:** Website styling and design.
- **JavaScript:** Website functionality.
- **Python:** Backend development.
- **MySQL:** Database management.

## 2. Methodology and Process for Implementation

1. Create a user-friendly website.
2. Allow farmers to register and upload products.
3. Allow customers to view and order products.
4. Connect farmers directly with customers.
5. Manage orders and delivery details.

## Feasibility and Viability

Our Direct Farm project is simple, affordable, and easy to develop. It can be implemented using basic web technologies. Farmers and customers can easily access the platform through a mobile phone or computer.

The project can be expanded in the future by adding online payments, AI-based demand forecasting, and delivery tracking.
